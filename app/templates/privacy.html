Privacy Charter

RoastLab is committed to the absolute privacy and security of your proprietary ideas. We utilize a zero-knowledge processing framework designed to protect your intellectual property from ingestion or storage.

1. Transient Data Ingestion

Startup concepts and pitch briefs are processed in a transient sandbox enclave. Your data is never stored on a central database; it remains in-memory only for the duration of the analysis.

2. Local Storage Enclaves

All saved verdicts are cryptographically anchored to your local browser storage or your private user profile. We do not have access to your private vault contents.

3. Zero Model Training

We do not use your inputs to train or fine-tune our models. Your strategic frameworks belong to you, and we ensure they stay that way.
