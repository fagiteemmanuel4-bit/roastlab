Audit Protocol

The RoastLab Audit Protocol (TAP) is our proprietary framework for evaluating startup viability through four distinct sequential phases.

  • Phase 1: Concept Breakdown - Feasibility and mechanical analysis.
  • Phase 2: Critical Analysis - Identification of structural unit-economic flaws.
  • Phase 3: Strategic Reconstruction - Actionable alternative strategies.
  • Phase 4: Performance Metrics - Scoring of market readiness and execution.
