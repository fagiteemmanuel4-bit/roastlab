Model Training Exclusion Charter

// DATA SOVEREIGNTY GUARANTEE

This charter represents our legally ironclad commitment isolating inference sandboxes from training mechanics. We assure all users that text nodes and PDF uploads are strictly banned from LLM ingestion loops.

Your inputs are never used to train, fine-tune, or otherwise improve our models or any third-party models. RoastLab is an inference-only environment designed for objective analysis, not data harvesting.
