Model Charter

This charter outlines the strict operational boundaries of the RoastLab AI core. We maintain a clear distinction between inference operations and training data loops.

Our models are pre-trained on vast datasets of business literature, unit economic patterns, and historical market failures. However, they are strictly prohibited from learning from user-provided inputs during live analysis sessions.
