Our Manifesto

At RoastLab, we believe that true growth happens at the intersection of honesty and expertise. Modern entrepreneurship is often clouded by "validation theatre" — the tendency for peers and mentors to provide soft, encouraging feedback that masks critical structural flaws.

We exist to break that cycle. Our mission is to provide founders with unvarnished, algorithmic validation. By identifying points of failure before they become catastrophic, we protect your time, your engineering resources, and your capital.

Our focus remains on the hard metrics of business: unit economics, regulatory feasibility, and market execution. We are the objective standard for launch readiness.
